OUR STORY

BORN FROM FIRE, CRAFTED WITH PASSION

The journey of bringing authentic culinary warmth to the city of Kandy.

FROM A SPARK TO A FLAME

Pepper by Pats started with a simple belief: that wood-fired cooking has an unmatched ability to bring out the deepest, most complex flavors in food. Our journey began in Kandy, driven by the desire to bring authentic, artisanal pizzas to our local community.

Every ingredient is chosen with purpose, and every recipe is crafted with care. Our signature 48-hour fermented sourdough is the foundation of everything we do, creating a crust that is both light and full of character.

THE SECRET IN THE SMOKE

Our wood-fired oven is the beating heart of our kitchen. Burning at temperatures over 450°C, it chars our pizzas in under 90 seconds, sealing in moisture and creating our signature smoky flavor.

We use a curated blend of local hardwoods to impart a unique aroma that defines the Pepper by Pats experience, blending traditional techniques with modern culinary finesse.

MEET THE CRAFTSMEN

Led by Pat, our team of passionate chefs is dedicated to preserving the art of traditional cooking. From hand-stretching the dough to monitoring the flames, we pour our hearts into every dish we serve.
